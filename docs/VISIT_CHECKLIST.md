# Duck Pond visit checklist

Things the model guessed. On a visit, note what's really there, and photos
help most: a wide shot from a known spot, and close-ups of details. Anything
not on this list came from measured data and is probably close.

## Around the water

- [ ] **Benches.** Most of the 52 are guessed, every ~45 m along the paths
      near the water, facing it. Where are the real ones, and which way do
      they face? What are they made of?
- [ ] **Lamp posts.** 37 guessed, every ~35 m. Where are they, how tall, what
      style of lantern?
- [ ] **Bins, picnic tables, the shelter.** Where, and what do they look like?
- [ ] **Paths.** Surface (asphalt? brick? gravel?) and width. The model
      paints them as brick-textured pavement, a little wider than real.
- [ ] **Stones and plants at the water's edge.** Where is it stones, where
      grass to the edge, where iris or reeds?

## Water

- [ ] **The weir.** How many steps, how wide, the walkway slabs across it?
      (The model has four concrete tiers.)
- [ ] **The islands.** Anything on them besides trees: rocks, a duck house?
- [ ] **Depth.** Any sign of how deep the pond is? (Guessed: 2 m at most.)
- [ ] **Fountains or aerators** in the water?

## Bridges and structures

- [ ] **The five bridges.** Wood or concrete, railings, length. Photos of
      each. (Modelled: rustic timber footbridges; Duck Pond Drive's with
      Hokie Stone walls, which is a pure guess.)
- [ ] **A gazebo?** Is there one near the pond, and where?
- [ ] **Signs, plaques, memorial stones.**

## Trees

- [ ] **Which are willows?** The model makes every tree within ~12 m of the
      water a weeping willow. Which of those are something else?
- [ ] **Evergreens.** Where are the conifers? The model guessed from the
      survey (mostly along the golf course).
- [ ] **Anything the lidar missed:** trees planted after 2017, or ones gone
      since.

## Birds

- [ ] **What lives there.** Mallards, Muscovy ducks and Chinese geese are
      modelled. Canada geese? Swans? Others? Roughly how many?

## Buildings (deferred)

- [ ] **Solitude**: a front photo, and the back.
- [ ] The rest can wait: the building pass is deferred.
